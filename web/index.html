Public prices

Download prices for every item in every city from the Albion Data Project, a free site built from other players' scans. Takes about 3 minutes. Public prices can be a few hours old and don't say how many are on offer, so they're marked public. Your own prices win when they're newer.

Record game traffic

Saves 2 minutes of raw game traffic to a file next to the sniffer, so Claude can find new things to read, like your carry weight.

  1. Press Start recording.
  2. Mount up and open your inventory.
  3. Write down the weight it shows, like 450 / 1,200 kg.
  4. Open the market and browse a few items.

The file can include your character name and nearby chat. It stays on your PC unless you send it.
